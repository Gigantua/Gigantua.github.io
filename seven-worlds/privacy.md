# Privacy Policy — Seven Worlds: Neon Odyssey

**Developer:** Gigantua  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-29

## Summary

Seven Worlds: Neon Odyssey does **not** collect, store, share or sell any personal data.
The game has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting service and no server of its own.
Everything the game saves stays on your device.

## Data stored on your device only

The game keeps the following purely locally (in the app's private storage) so it can work as a game:

- Your high score and game progress
- Your settings (e.g. volume, language, controls)
- A local `crash.log` file in the app cache if the game crashes, to help you report a problem — it is never sent anywhere automatically

This data never leaves your device and is removed when you uninstall the app (or clear the app's data). We have no access to it.

## Data we do not collect

We do not collect or transmit: names, e-mail addresses, location, contacts, device identifiers, advertising IDs, usage statistics or gameplay telemetry. The game does not use cookies or tracking technologies.

## In-app purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed entirely by the store you installed the game from:

- **Google Play** — Google Play Billing
- **Amazon Appstore** — Amazon In-App Purchasing
- **Microsoft Store** — Microsoft Store services
- **Apple App Store** — StoreKit in-app purchase
- **Meta Horizon Store (Meta Quest)** — Meta in-app purchases
- **Epic Games Store, LG TV and the direct Windows download** — PayPal (payment is completed on PayPal's website in your browser)

The store or payment provider handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. The game only receives confirmation that a purchase was made (a purchase/entitlement status) so it can unlock the purchased content; it never receives your payment information.

- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Amazon: https://www.amazon.com/privacy
- Microsoft: https://privacy.microsoft.com
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Meta: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/
- PayPal: https://www.paypal.com/legalhub/privacy-full

## Network permission

The Android app declares the `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` permissions solely so the store's billing library can complete purchases. The game itself makes no other network requests.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Third-party services

Apart from the store billing services listed above, the game integrates no third-party SDKs that process personal data.

## Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts to collect data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted here with a new effective date.
